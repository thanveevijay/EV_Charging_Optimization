# Fairness Evaluation

Average waiting time can hide poor service for a subset of sessions. Consider reporting:

- Median and 90th-percentile waiting time.
- Completion rate grouped by arrival hour.
- Energy fulfillment grouped by session duration.
- Waiting time grouped by requested-energy band, when requested energy exists.
- Share of sessions that depart without completing.

Group comparisons should use sufficiently large samples and disclose group definitions. Avoid using sensitive personal attributes; station and time-based operational groupings are generally more appropriate for this simulator.
