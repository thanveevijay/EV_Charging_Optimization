# Known Limitations

1. Delivered energy may be used as a proxy for demand; it is not necessarily the driver's requested energy.
2. Historical ACN sessions may not represent future stations, vehicle populations, or user behavior.
3. Simulated charger availability and power assumptions may differ from physical infrastructure.
4. Reward weights encode design choices and can change policy behavior.
5. A graph model's performance depends on graph construction and feature quality.
6. Stochastic training can produce run-to-run variation.
7. A lower simulated waiting time does not automatically imply lower real-world operating cost.

These limitations should accompany charts and performance claims.
